About

Built by devotees, for devotees.

Sadhana App is made by GlitGrace in Visnagar, Gujarat, India.

Why we built it.

Daily practice should not need five apps and a pile of ads. We wanted one calm place for mantras, aarti, japa and meditation, in the language you pray in.

Sadhana is free to start. An optional Premium plan keeps the app ad-light and funds new content — and a fixed ₹10 from every subscription payment goes to charity.

Your languageTen Indian languages, from Gujarati to Malayalam.
Calm by designNo clutter, no noise, nothing in the way of your practice.
Respect for your dataWe never store card or UPI details. You can delete your account any time.
Seva built in₹10 from every subscription is donated, with receipts published.
