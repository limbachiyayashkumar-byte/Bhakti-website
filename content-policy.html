Content Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026 · Applies to Sadhana App (package com.yash.bhakti), a GlitGrace product

1. Introduction

Sadhana App is a devotional companion from GlitGrace. This Content Policy explains how we create, curate and host mantras, aarti, bhajans, wallpapers, audiobooks and other material inside the app, and what you can do if something feels incorrect, inappropriate or otherwise concerning.

By using Sadhana App you agree to this policy along with our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2. AI-assisted content

Some content in Sadhana App may be generated or assisted by artificial intelligence, and some may not. We use AI tools carefully to help with translation, transcription, formatting, and presentation of traditional material so that devotees can access it in more languages and formats.

Important: AI-assisted text or audio is always reviewed with the intention of remaining faithful to traditional sources. It is provided for personal devotion and learning — not as a substitute for guidance from a qualified priest, guru or scholar.

We do not claim that every line is AI-free, nor that every line is AI-generated. Both human-curated and AI-assisted content may appear side by side in the app.

3. Sources & authenticity

Traditional mantras, slokas and aarti belong to the shared cultural and religious heritage of India. We draw from widely known public-domain and traditionally recited texts. Where we provide translations or transliterations, our goal is clarity and respect, not doctrinal authority.

If you notice an error in a mantra, translation, attribution or audio, please tell us — we welcome corrections from the community.

4. Community uploads

When you upload audio (for example for the Ringtone section) or other material, you confirm that you have the right to share it and that it is suitable for a devotional audience. We may review, decline or remove any submission at our discretion.

5. Prohibited content

Content that is not welcome in Sadhana App includes, but is not limited to:

  • Hate speech, harassment or content that demeans any community, caste, gender or faith
  • Sexually explicit, violent or otherwise non-devotional material
  • Copyright-infringing uploads where the uploader does not hold the necessary rights
  • Misinformation presented as religious authority or medical/financial advice
  • Spam, malware links or attempts to phish other users

6. Report an issue

If you have any concern about content in Sadhana App — whether it involves AI assistance, accuracy, attribution, community uploads or anything else — please write to us. We take reports seriously and aim to respond promptly.

Email us: support@glitgrace.in
Include a short description of the content, where you saw it (screen or section if possible), and why you are flagging it. Screenshots help.

Email support@glitgrace.in

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Content Policy as the app and our practices evolve. The “Effective date” at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of Sadhana App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
