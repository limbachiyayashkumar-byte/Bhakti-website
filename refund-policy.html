Refund Policy

Effective date: 10 August 2026 · Applies to paid subscriptions within Sadhana App, a GlitGrace product

Summary Free trial, first No-refund policy Exceptions Purchases through Google Play How to cancel Changes to this policy Contact us

1. Summary

Every paid plan in Sadhana App starts with a free trial. You will never be charged without first having the chance to try the plan and cancel before billing begins. Because of this, we do not offer refunds for subscription charges once a billing period has started, except where required by law or described below.

2. Free trial, first — always

Before any paid subscription begins charging you, Sadhana App gives you a free trial period, clearly shown at the time of purchase along with the exact date charging will begin. You can explore the premium features risk-free and decide, before you're charged, whether the plan is right for you.

3. No-refund policy

Because the free trial already gives you a full opportunity to evaluate the subscription before being charged, once a billing period has started and payment has been successfully processed, that charge is final and non-refundable — including if you forget to cancel before the trial ends, use the app for only part of the billing period, or decide later that you no longer want the subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not refund the period already paid for.

4. Exceptions

We will review a refund request, at our discretion, in the following limited situations:

  • You were charged more than once for the same billing period due to a technical error
  • You were charged after successfully cancelling before the trial ended
  • A payment was made fraudulently, without your authorisation, on your payment instrument
  • The subscription is required to be refunded under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other applicable Indian law

To raise any of these, email support@glitgrace.in with your account email and payment reference within 7 days of the charge.

5. Purchases made through Google Play

If your subscription was purchased through the Google Play Store, the charge is processed by Google, not directly by GlitGrace. Google Play's own refund policy and window may separately apply to your purchase, alongside this policy. You can request a refund directly from Google at support.google.com/googleplay or through your Google Play purchase history. GlitGrace does not control the outcome of refund requests submitted directly to Google.

6. How to cancel your subscription

You can cancel anytime before your next billing date to avoid future charges:

  • On Google Play: Open the Google Play Store app → Menu → Subscriptions → Sadhana App → Cancel subscription
  • In the app: Account Settings → Manage Subscription

Cancelling keeps your paid features active until the end of the period you've already paid for; it will not renew after that.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy as our subscription plans evolve. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest version. Please review it periodically.
