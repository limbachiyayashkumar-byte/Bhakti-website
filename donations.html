1. Our pledge

From every subscription payment made in Sadhana App, GlitGrace donates a fixed ₹10 to good causes. It is part of the subscription price — you never pay extra for it.

2. How it works

  • Each successful subscription payment adds ₹10 to our donation fund.
  • We donate the collected amount to one or more of the causes below, then publish the details and receipt on this page.
  • The ₹4 trial fee is not counted; the ₹10 applies to subscription payments.

3. Causes we support

"Good cause" means real, needy work that helps living beings and society. Each cycle we pick from the areas below and name the exact organisation on this page.

Gau seva

Fodder, shelter and medical care for cows in gaushalas.

Animal welfare

Food, water and treatment for stray and injured animals and birds.

Education

Fees, books and supplies for children who cannot afford them.

Anna daan

Meals for the hungry, temple kitchens and community food drives.

Healthcare

Medical help and medicines for people in need.

Environment

Tree planting and care for local water and green spaces.

For every donation we publish the cause, the organisation, the amount and the date below. The first donation details will be added soon.
