Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Bhakti App is developed and operated by GlitGrace ("GlitGrace", "we", "us", "our"), based in Visnagar, Gujarat, India. This policy explains what information Bhakti App collects, why we collect it, and how it is stored and protected, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play's Data Safety requirements.

By creating an account or using Bhakti App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication shares your Google account's name, email address and profile photo with us so we can identify your account and sync your data across devices. You can also use parts of the app without signing in.

Content you create in the app

  • Your selected deity, language and onboarding preferences
  • Favourites, saved mantras, and japa mala round counts
  • Audio recordings you choose to upload for the community Ringtone section, along with the name you attribute them to
  • Wallpaper and alarm/reminder preferences

Automatically collected information

  • Basic usage and diagnostic data via Firebase Analytics (screens viewed, feature usage, crash reports) — used only in aggregate to improve the app
  • Device and app-integrity signals via Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check, used to confirm requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app
What we don't collect: Bhakti App does not access your contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone or precise location, and we do not run third-party advertising trackers inside the app.

4. How we use your information

  • To operate core features: multilingual mantras, aarti/bhajan playback, wallpapers, audiobooks, alarms and the daily-sloka widget
  • To keep your favourites, japa counts and preferences in sync across your devices
  • To moderate and, once approved, display audio you choose to share with other devotees
  • To detect abuse, secure accounts, and keep the service reliable
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve the app

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to serve third-party advertising profiles.

5. Community audio uploads

If you submit a devotional recording for the Ringtone section, it is reviewed before it becomes visible to other users, and is shown together with the name you provide for attribution ("Uploaded by ..."). Please only upload audio you have the right to share, and keep submissions devotional in nature. We may remove any upload, and may use your contact details to reach you about it.

6. Sharing & third-party services

We share information only with the service providers that power the app, each acting under their own privacy and security commitments:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage (audio and images), Analytics, and App Check/Play Integrity
  • Google Play Services — sign-in and core Android services

We do not share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of GlitGrace, our users, or the public.

7. Storage & security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure, which may process and store data on servers located outside India as part of Google's global cloud network. Firebase applies industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access to our backend is restricted, and Firestore/Storage security rules only allow reads and writes appropriate to a signed-in user. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we remove your personal data and app content within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain limited records for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

9. Your rights & choices

You may, at any time:

  • Sign out or switch Google accounts from Account Settings in the app
  • Request a copy of the personal data linked to your account
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for uses that depend on it, by contacting us or deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@glitgrace.in. We will respond within a reasonable time, consistent with the DPDP Act, 2023.

10. Children's privacy

Bhakti App is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective date" above, and where appropriate, an in-app notice. Continued use of Bhakti App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
