Support

How can we help?

Quick answers below. If you are stuck, write to us.

How do I change my deity or language?

Use the app’s settings to change your ishta devta and language at any time.

How do I set a ringtone or alarm tone?

Open any ringtone and tap “Set Ringtone” or “Set Alarm”. This needs Premium and the system permission to modify settings, which the app will ask for.

How do I add the daily sloka widget?

Long-press your home screen, choose Widgets, find “Daily Mantra & Sloka” under Sadhana and drag it in.

I’m not getting reminders or darshan notifications.

Allow notifications for Sadhana in your phone settings (required on Android 13 and above) and exclude the app from aggressive battery saving on some phones.

Audio or images are not loading.

Content streams from our servers, so check your connection. Once an audio file has played it is cached for faster replays.

How do I delete my account and data?

Follow the steps on our Delete Account & Data page.

Will I be charged automatically?

Yes. Google Play renews your subscription each month at the price shown in the app unless you cancel. Any introductory offer is shown on the subscription screen before you subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Open Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sadhana → Cancel. Premium access continues until the end of the paid period.

Is payment secure?

Yes. All billing is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

I already paid but still see limits.

Tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen, or wait a few seconds after purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Google account used to buy.

Still need help?

Email support@glitgrace.in with your Google account email and a short description (a screenshot helps).
