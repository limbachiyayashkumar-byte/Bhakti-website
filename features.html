Features

Everything for your daily practice.

Sadhana App brings the whole routine — chanting, listening, meditating and counting — into one calm, ad-light app.

ॐ

Mantras & slokas

Read and listen to mantras and slokas for your chosen deity, including the Bhagavad Gita, in ten Indian languages.

  • Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Marathi, Kannada, Bengali, Punjabi, English
  • Mantra detail sheet with audio
  • Save favourites for one-tap access

Aarti, bhajans & ringtones

Curated devotional audio streamed from our library, with a mini-player that keeps playing in the background.

  • Media-style notification controls
  • Set a track as ringtone or alarm tone (Premium)
  • Reel-style ringtone browsing

Japa mala counter

A full-screen bead counter you can tap anywhere. Choose a traditional count or set your own, and watch your rounds add up.

  • Tap anywhere to count a bead
  • Custom bead target per mala
  • Rounds completed, with a gentle mala-complete cue

Guided meditation

Sessions with a breathing rhythm to follow and optional ambient sound.

  • 5 min calm, 10 min deep, 15 min release
  • 7 min breath focus, or free flow with no timer
  • Choose a background sound

Devotional audiobooks

Long-form narrations, episode by episode, with a full-screen player and adjustable speed.

  • First episode of every audiobook is free
  • Full library with Premium

Divine wallpapers

High-resolution devotional art organised by deity, with a preview before you apply it.

  • Set for home screen, lock screen or both
  • Save to gallery
  • Unlock any wallpaper with Premium

Daily Mahakal Darshan

A fresh darshan image of Mahakal every day, with a gentle reminder in the morning.

  • Full-screen view
  • Share with family and friends
  • Share without ads on Premium

Daily status feed

A swipeable feed of the day’s devotional status — wallpapers and ringtones — ready to save or share on WhatsApp.

  • Swipe up to continue
  • Save to gallery
  • Share in one tap

Widget, reminders & alarms

A daily sloka widget for your home screen and notifications that bring you back to your practice.

  • Home-screen Daily Mantra & Sloka widget
  • Devotional reminders you control
  • Alerts when new content is added

Choose your ishta devta

Nine deities, one home screen.

Pick the deity closest to your heart and the app tunes its mantras, aarti and wallpapers around them.

Begin today’s sādhanā.

Sign in with Google, pick your deity and language, and your practice is ready in under a minute.
