Questions

Premium FAQ.

What is Sadhana Premium?

Premium removes limits on wallpapers, ringtones and audiobooks and turns off ads, so you get the full devotional experience.

Will I be charged automatically?

Yes. Google Play renews your subscription each month at the price shown in the app unless you cancel. Any introductory offer is shown on the subscription screen before you subscribe.

How do I cancel?

Open Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sadhana → Cancel. Premium access continues until the end of the paid period.

Is payment secure?

Yes. All billing is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

I already paid but still see limits.

Tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen, or wait a few seconds after purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Google account used to buy.

Can I use Premium on multiple devices?

Yes, on any device signed in with the Google account that purchased the subscription.

Where does the ₹10 donation go?

To good causes such as gau seva, animal welfare and education. Details and receipts are on our Donation page.
